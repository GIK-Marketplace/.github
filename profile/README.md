# GIK Marketplace

**Moving at the Speed of Need.**

There are plenty of resources to go around. The problem is getting them into the right hands, where they can do the most good. That is the reason we exist.

GIK ([gik.org](https://gik.org)) is a centralized marketplace that moves vital resources from providers to responders with efficiency and transparency. AI-powered matching connects corporate surplus goods with verified nonprofit needs in real time, for disaster relief and community response, coordinated alongside logistics and government partners.

## What the Marketplace Does

- **Smart Matching:** AI connects surplus resources with verified needs in real time
- **Full Transparency:** every contribution is tracked from proposal to delivery
- **Tax Documentation:** automated records for boards and regulators
- **100% Free to Use:** no fees for suppliers or nonprofits

## Who It Serves

| Partner | Role |
|---|---|
| **Corporate Suppliers** | Put surplus inventory to work where it does the most good |
| **Nonprofits** | Post verified needs and receive matched resources |
| **Logistics Partners** | Move resources from origin to responder |
| **Government Partners** | Accountability, compliance, and coordination |

## Built for AI Assistants Too

The marketplace ships an AI connector built on the Model Context Protocol (MCP), so an organization's AI assistant can search verified needs, manage surplus resources, make and track offers, and follow orders through delivery, all scoped to that organization's own account.
